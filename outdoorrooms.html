Outdoor Rooms FAQ

What makes an outdoor room different from a normal patio?

An outdoor room usually combines more than one element — such as paving, shade, seating, cooking space and planting — so the finished area feels usable rather than just open space.

Can you build a full outdoor living area from scratch?

Yes. We can help plan and install the patio base, adjoining features and wider garden works needed to create a complete outdoor living space.
